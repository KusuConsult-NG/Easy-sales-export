# Easy Sales Export: Platform Training & Walkthrough Guide
*Written in simple, clear language so that anyone—even a 7-year-old—can understand and use the entire application without guessing.*

---

## 🌟 SECTION 1: The User Hub (Your Home Base)

The **User Hub** is the very first screen you see after logging in. Think of it as your personal farm office. It acts as the central control room for your entire account.

### 1. Dashboard Overview (What is on the Screen)
When you log in, look at the screen. You will see:
* **The Welcome Banner:** A dark blue card at the top that says *"Hello, [Your Name]!"*
* **The Stats Row:** Three white cards that show:
  * **Wallet Balance:** How much money you have.
  * **Unread Messages:** A counter showing how many new messages you have in your inbox.
  * **Notifications:** A counter showing how many new alerts you have.
* **Platform Modules Grid:** A collection of 6 colorful cards (Academy, WAVE, Export Windows, Marketplace, Cooperatives, and Farm Nation). Clicking any card takes you straight into that module.
* **Quick Access Bar:** Small shortcut icons at the bottom to quickly jump to your Wallet or Notifications page.

---

### 2. The Digital Wallet (Managing Your Money)
Your Digital Wallet is your digital piggy bank inside the app. It holds your funds for buying products, making savings, or receiving payments from sales.

#### A. How to Check Your Balance
1. Look at your Hub dashboard.
2. Find the card labeled **"Wallet Balance"** (it has a green card icon 💳).
3. The amount displayed in Naira (₦) is your current balance.

#### B. How to Deposit Money (Adding Cash)
1. Click the **"Wallet"** shortcut button.
2. Click the green button labeled **"Deposit Funds"**.
3. Type the amount of money you want to add (e.g., `10000` for ₦10,000).
4. Click **"Proceed to Payment"**. A secure checkout window from **Paystack** will pop up on your screen.
5. Choose how you want to pay:
   * **Pay with Card:** Enter your debit card details and your card PIN when prompted.
   * **Pay with Bank Transfer:** Copy the temporary bank account number shown on screen, open your bank app, transfer the exact amount, and then click *"I have sent the money"* in the Paystack window.
   * **Pay with USSD:** Select your bank and dial the displayed code on your phone.
6. Once Paystack confirms the transfer, the pop-up window will close, and your new balance will appear in your wallet.

#### C. How to Withdraw Money (Cashing Out)
1. Go to your Wallet dashboard.
2. Click the blue button labeled **"Request Withdrawal"**.
3. Type the amount you want to withdraw.
4. Select your bank from the dropdown list and type your bank account number.
5. Click **"Submit Withdrawal Request"**.
6. The status will show as **"Pending"** (Yellow). Once the Admin team approves the transaction, the money will be sent to your real bank account, and the status will turn to **"Completed"** (Green).

---

### 3. Profile & KYC Identity Check
To keep the community safe, you must prove you are a real person before you can sell items, apply for cooperative loans, or reserve land.

#### How to Verify Your Account:
1. Click on your profile image or name at the very top right of the screen.
2. Click **"Verify Identity"**.
3. You will see the **QoreID Identity Check Box**.
4. Choose whether you want to verify using your **NIN** (National ID) or **BVN** (Bank Verification Number).
5. Type your number in the field.
6. Upload a clear photo of your ID card:
   * Click **"Upload Document"**.
   * Choose the photo from your phone or computer. Make sure there are no shadows and that your name is easy to read.
7. Click the green **"Submit KYC"** button.
8. Your account status badge will turn yellow and say **"Pending Review"**. Once the admin approves, it will turn green and say **"Verified"**.

---

### 4. Notifications & Messaging Center
* **Notification Bell:** Located in the top navigation bar. Click the bell icon 🔔 to see a list of recent updates (e.g., *"Order #104 has shipped"* or *"Loan Approved"*).
* **Messages (The Chatroom):** Click the envelope icon ✉️ in the top bar. Here you can type messages and chat in real-time with sellers, buyers, or customer support.

### 1. Digital Export Marketplace
The Marketplace is a B2B agricultural shop where users buy and sell high-grade **Yam Tubers**, **Sesame Seeds**, and **Dried Hibiscus**.

#### 🛒 A. For Buyers: How to Buy Products
1. Click the **Marketplace** card on the Hub.
2. **Search & Filter:** Type what you need in the search bar, or use the dropdown filters to show items from a specific state or with a high seller rating.
3. **Select Your Quantity & Pricing Tier:**
   * Look at the product page. The price drops automatically based on how much you buy:
     * **Retail Tier:** Smallest order volume (highest price per unit).
     * **Bulk Tier:** Medium order volume (discounted price).
     * **Export Tier:** Large tonnage volume (lowest wholesale price).
   * Type your quantity, and the system will automatically apply the correct price tier.
4. **Add to Cart & Checkout:**
   * Click **"Add to Basket"**.
   * Click the cart icon at the top right, review your items, and click **"Proceed to Checkout"**.
   * Enter your delivery address and contact phone number.
   * Click **"Pay with Wallet"** to confirm.
5. **The Escrow Safety Check:**
   * When you pay, the money does **not** go to the seller. The app holds it safely in an **Escrow Lockbox**.
   * The seller will see that payment is secured and will ship your crops.
   * Once the truck arrives, inspect your crops. If they are in good condition, go to your **Orders** page and click the green button labeled **"Confirm Delivery"**. This releases the escrow money to the seller's wallet.
6. **How to Raise a Dispute (If crops are bad):**
   * If your crops arrive spoiled, wet, or damaged, do **not** click "Confirm Delivery".
   * Instead, click the red button labeled **"Raise Dispute"** on the order page.
   * Type the reason for your dispute and upload photo proof. Click **"Submit Dispute"**. This freezes the money in escrow and alerts an Admin to step in.

---

#### 🧑‍🌾 B. For Sellers: How to Sell Products
1. Click **Marketplace** and go to the **Seller Portal**.
2. **Complete the 5-Stage Seller KYC:**
   * Complete your profile details, upload your CAC company registration certificate, verify your ID via QoreID, wait for Admin approval, and customize your storefront name.
3. **List Your Product:**
   * Click the blue button labeled **"Create Listing"**.
   * Upload high-quality photos of your crops.
   * Fill out the form fields: select the commodity type, write a title, type the **Moisture Content (%)** (e.g., `6%`), specify the **Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)**, select the **Origin State**, and type your prices for the Retail, Bulk, and Export tiers.
   * Click **"Publish Listing"**.
4. **Track Your Orders (Order Status Stages):**
   * Go to **"Active Orders"** to manage your sales. You must update the order status as you work:
     * `pending_payment`: The buyer is checking out.
     * `payment_secured`: The buyer paid into Escrow. You can now package the crops!
     * `processing`: You are sorting, cleaning, and packing the crops.
     * `in_transit`: The crops are on the truck. Type the tracking info and click **"Ship"**.
     * `delivered`: The crops arrived at the buyer's location.
     * `completed`: The buyer clicked "Confirm Delivery", and the funds are moved to your wallet.

### 2. Cooperatives (Savings & Loans Club)
The Cooperative is a savings club where members pool funds to earn rewards and access credit.

#### A. How to Join
1. Click the **Cooperatives** card on the Hub.
2. You will see a prompt to pay the **One-Time Membership Registration Fee** of **₦10,000**.
3. Click the purple button labeled **"Pay Registration Fee"**.
4. Pay the ₦10,000 via Paystack. Once verified, your account is upgraded to **Member** status and the Cooperative dashboard unlocks.

#### B. Savings Contributions
To build borrowing power, you must contribute savings.
* Contributions must be **₦5,000 and above** per deposit.
1. Click **"Add Contribution"** on the Cooperative dashboard.
2. Type the amount you want to save.
3. Click **"Contribute Now"** to transfer the funds from your Digital Wallet to your Cooperative Savings account.

#### C. Goal Folders and Locked Savings
1. Click **"Create Savings Goal"**.
2. Name your goal (e.g., "Seed Capital") and set a target amount.
3. Select a lock duration: **1, 3, 6, or 12 months**.
4. Click **"Launch Goal"**.
   * ⚠️ **Warning:** If you withdraw your savings before the lock timer ends, you will pay an early-withdrawal penalty fee. If you wait until the maturity date, you receive your savings plus a **10% interest bonus**!

#### D. Borrowing Loans
1. Click **"Apply for Loan"**.
2. The system calculates your maximum borrowing limit automatically:
   $$\text{Maximum Loan Amount} = \text{Your Total Savings} \times 3$$
   *Example: If you have saved ₦50,000, you can borrow up to ₦150,000.*
3. Type the requested amount and choose a repayment period (up to 12 months).
4. Click **"Submit Application"**. Once the Admin approves, the cash is deposited into your wallet. The dashboard will display your monthly repayment tracker with a 2% monthly interest breakdown.

#### E. Checkout using Cooperative Credit
* When buying seeds or tools in the Marketplace, select **"Pay with Cooperative Credit"** at checkout to use your cooperative balance directly.

#### F. Membership ID Card
* Click the **"ID Card"** menu tab on your Cooperative dashboard to view and download your official Cooperative Member ID Card.

### 3. Easy Sales Export Academy (LMS)
The Academy is a digital school that teaches you how to export commodities.

1. **Course Enrollment:** Click **Academy** on the Hub, browse the available courses, and click **"Start Course"**.
2. **Sequential Lesson Progression:**
   * Click Lesson 1. You must watch the video and read the lesson text fully.
   * ⚠️ **Next Lesson Lock:** You cannot skip ahead to Lesson 2 or the final quiz. The app monitors your watch time and will keep subsequent lessons locked until the current lesson is completed.
3. **Assessment Quizzes:**
   * At the end of the course, click **"Take Final Quiz"**.
   * **Timer Check:** A countdown clock will tick at the top of the quiz page. If you leave the browser tab or if the timer hits `00:00`, your quiz is automatically submitted.
4. **Certificate Generation & Verification:**
   * If you pass the final quiz with a score of **95% or higher**, click the gold button labeled **"Download Certificate"**. The page will generate a high-quality PDF in your browser.
   * **Verification Link:** Anyone can scan the QR code on your certificate to view your public student page at `/academy/verify/[certificateId]` to prove it is authentic.
   * **LinkedIn Sharing:** Click **"Add to LinkedIn"** to automatically add the certificate to your LinkedIn profile.

### 4. Farm Nation (Real Estate & Agrarian Land)
Farm Nation helps cooperative members buy or lease verified agricultural land plots.

1. Click **Farm Nation** on the Hub.
2. **Search Listings:** Filter plots by State, Acreage, and **Crop Viability** (e.g., search for land best suited for "Root Crops" or "Grains").
3. **Initiating Purchase:**
   * Click on a plot listing.
   * Click **"Initiate Purchase"**.
   * Read the **Zoning and Land Use Agreement** checkbox. Check the box to agree to use the land strictly for farming.
   * Click **"Lock Land Reservation"** to secure the deposit in Escrow.
   * An Admin inspector will visit the plot to check boundaries. Once verified, the title deed is signed over to you, and the seller is paid.

### 5. WAVE Program (Women's Empowerment Portal)
WAVE is an empowerment program for female agro-processors.

1. **Gate Guard:** Access to this portal is restricted. The system checks your profile details. If your gender is set to **Male**, you will be blocked and see an *"Access Restricted"* page.
2. **Announcements Board:** View upcoming grant details, processing workshop locations, and regional empowerment events.
3. **Resource Library:** Click **"Resource Library"** to download guides for starting a processing plant, SME loan applications, and export checklists.

### 6. Export Logistics Engine
When you are ready to ship your commodities out of Nigeria, book an export slot!

1. Go to the **Export Dashboard** and click **"Book Export Slot"**.
2. **Follow the 5-Stage Booking Wizard:**
   * **Stage 1 (Commodity):** Select Yam, Sesame, or Hibiscus and enter your total weight in metric tons.
   * **Stage 2 (Specs):** Enter your crop specifications: moisture percentage, foreign matter percentage, and confirm that you have a Phytosanitary Certificate.
   * **Stage 3 (Logistics):** Select your shipping terms (FOB or CIF), choose your Port of Origin (e.g., "Lagos Apapa Port"), and select your Cargo Vessel.
   * **Stage 4 (Documents):** Upload photo copies of your *Bill of Lading* and *Certificate of Origin*.
   * **Stage 5 (Tax Settlement):** Review computed port duties and export taxes. Click **"Pay and Confirm Slot"**.
3. **The Export Calendar:** Your dashboard will now render a calendar showing your cargo drop-off date, ship loading days, and vessel departure date.

## 🎛️ SECTION 3: The Admin Command Center (For Supervisors)

The Admin Portal (located at `/admin`) is the control room of the platform. It is a secure, zero-trust environment where every administrative action is logged.

---

### 1. Analytics & Telemetry Dashboard (`/admin/analytics`)
Use this page to check the health of the platform.
* **Core KPI Metric Cards:** Display active users, total savings, active loan volumes, and pending approvals.
  * 🔄 **Live Server Counting:** These numbers use Firestore `count()` aggregations to scan the database. While loading, you will see a spinner. Once loaded, they show the exact totals across the entire platform.
* **Telemetry Charts:**
  * **Revenue Trend (Line Chart):** Maps platform commission earnings over the last 6 months.
  * **User Registrations (Bar Chart):** Shows the sign-up velocity of new users per month.
  * **Module Breakdown (Pie Chart):** Displays which module has the highest traffic.

---

### 2. Financial Auditing & Ledgers (`/admin/finance`)
Admins act as security guards for all financial transactions.

#### A. Approving Wallet Withdrawals
1. Go to the **Withdrawals** page.
2. Review the pending payout requests.
3. Click on a request to check details:
   * Confirm the user's KYC status is **Verified** (Green badge).
   * Confirm the bank account name matches the user's KYC name.
4. If correct, click **"Approve Payout"**. The system will process the Paystack transfer.
5. If there is a name mismatch, click the red **"Reject Payout"** button, select the reason from the dropdown, and click confirm.

#### B. Underwriting Cooperative Loans
1. Go to the **Cooperative Loans** page.
2. Select a pending loan application.
3. Review the loan underwriting report card:
   * **Total Saved:** The user's active savings contribution.
   * **Requested Amount:** The loan amount they want to borrow.
   * **Debt Ratio:** Checks if they have outstanding unpaid loans.
   * **Multiplier Check:** Confirms the request does not exceed 3x their savings.
4. Click **"Approve Loan Application"** to deposit the funds into their wallet, or click **"Deny Loan"** to reject the request.

### 3. The Communications Hub (`/admin/communications`)
Use this suite to broadcast news and alerts to users.

#### A. Segment Targeting (Choosing Your Audience)
Before sending a message, choose your audience in the dropdown list:
* **All Users:** Sends the message to every account.
* **Active Last 30 Days:** Targets only frequent users.
* **Fully Verified Sellers:** Targets only merchants.
* **WAVE Only:** Targets only female participants.
* **CSV Cohort Upload:** Drag and drop a CSV file containing user emails to target a specific custom list.

#### B. Creating Global Dashboard Banners
1. Type your announcement in the text field.
2. As you type, look at the **Live Preview Box** on the right. This shows exactly what the banner will look like on the user's screen!
3. Select a **Priority Level** (Color Theme):
   * 🟦 **Information (Blue):** For general news.
   * 🟩 **Success (Green):** For positive announcements.
   * 🟨 **Warning (Yellow):** For scheduled maintenance alerts.
   * 🟥 **Critical (Red):** For urgent alerts.
4. Click **"Publish Announcement"**. It will instantly appear at the top of all user dashboards!

### 4. User Directory & Bulk Operations (`/admin/users`)
This page displays the list of all registered members.

#### A. Cursor-Based Infinite Scroll
* Scroll down the page; more users will load automatically.
* The directory uses cursor-based pagination. This means that even if new users register while you are scrolling, you will not see duplicate records or skip anyone.

#### B. High-Velocity Bulk Actions
1. Select multiple users by checking the boxes on the left.
2. A floating **Bulk Actions Bar** will slide up from the bottom of your screen!
3. Click **"Approve KYC"** on the floating bar. The system will process all selected accounts at once, saving you time!

#### C. Exporting Datasets
* To download your current list of users, click the **"Export CSV"** button. 
* The system will download a `.csv` spreadsheet file to your computer containing the names, emails, phone numbers, and roles of the selected users.

### 5. The Rejection Modal (The Red Card)
Whenever you reject an application (like a loan, a seller profile, or land booking), you must use the **Rejection Modal**.

```
[ Admin Rejection Flow ]
 Click Reject Button → Modal Opens → Type Justification (10-500 chars) → Confirm Rejection (Submit Locked)
```

1. Click the **"Reject"** button on an application.
2. A modal window will pop up.
3. **Write a Justification:** You must type a reason explaining why the application is rejected (e.g., *"CAC Certificate copy is blurry and unreadable"*). 
   > ⚠️ **Length Rule:** Your text must be between 10 and 500 characters. You cannot leave this blank or type short words like "no".
4. Click **"Confirm Rejection"**. The submit button will display a loading spinner and lock itself to prevent double clicks. The user will receive an email and dashboard notification containing your reason.

### 6. Transparency Audit Logs & Edit Logging
* Go to the **Audit Logs** page to view a history of every administrative change.
* When an admin edits user details (like correcting a phone number), the system saves a snapshot showing:
  * The name of the Admin who made the change.
  * The old data value (**"Before"**).
  * The new data value (**"After"**).
  * These logs are read-only and cannot be edited or deleted by anyone (including admins) to guarantee accountability.

---

### 7. Feature Toggles Matrix (System Kill-Switches)
* Go to the **Feature Toggles** page.
* You will see a list of toggles for different app features:
  * `Loan Applications` (On/Off)
  * `Withdrawal Requests` (On/Off)
  * `Export Window Registrations` (On/Off)
* If there is an emergency (such as a bank checkout issue or a liquidity crunch), a Super Admin can toggle a switch to **"Off"**. 
* This immediately hides that feature from the user-facing app globally, preventing any new submissions without needing code changes!
